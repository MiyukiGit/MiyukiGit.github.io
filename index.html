About Me

I'm Affif Wafi bin Ismail, a scholarship student and aspiring software engineer. I'm drawn to technology because I enjoy taking messy or complicated problems and turning them into systems that are simple, structured, and useful.

During my time at Kolej MARA Kuala Nerang, I built a strong foundation in programming through coursework and personal projects, mainly working with C++ and Python. Since then, I've been exploring web and mobile development, AI, automation, and data visualisation through projects that solve practical, everyday problems. I don't just want my projects to 'work'. I want to understand 'why they work' and 'how to build them' properly.

I'm now pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Science (Hons.) in Software Engineering at Multimedia University (MMU). I'm particularly interested in software engineering, data science, AI, and building practical applications that solve everyday problems.

I'm still learning, experimenting, and breaking things along the way. But I believe in understanding the fundamentals rather than relying on shortcuts. I don't just want to build features. I want to understand the problem, find a solution, and build it properly.
